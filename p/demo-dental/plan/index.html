01 · What we found

Your Google presence today

  • 4.6★ rating on GoogleWorking

    Patients already rate you well. The opportunity is volume: more recent reviews so you rank higher for "dentist near me".

  • 74 reviews vs 412 at the busiest clinic nearbyCosting you patients

    Google favours clinics with more — and more recent — reviews. Asking every patient after their visit, automatically, closes this gap.

  • No website on your Google profileCosting you patients

    Patients who find you on Maps can't see your treatments, doctors or timings — so they call the clinic that shows them. The preview we built fixes this.

  • Booking only by phoneCosting you patients

    Calls during treatment or lunch go unanswered. A WhatsApp booking button lets patients book at 11 pm — and you confirm in the morning.

  • Reviews aren't being replied toCould be better

    Patients who call then simply try the next clinic. A WhatsApp booking button and an away message catch them instead.

03 · Your free toolkit

Copy, paste, use today

These are written for Sunrise Dental Studio. Replace anything in [brackets] and send. No strings attached.

Ask for a review (send the same day)

Hi [Name], thank you for visiting Sunrise Dental today! Could you take a minute to share your experience on Google? Honest feedback helps other patients find the right dentist: [review link]

— Dr. Meera Kulkarni

Get your review link: Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews". Send it to every patient, not just the happy ones — Google doesn't allow asking only for positive reviews. Also print it as a QR code for reception.

Reply to a 5★ review

Thank you so much, [Name]! It was a pleasure seeing you. We look forward to your next check-up.
— Team Sunrise Dental

Reply within 48 hours. Keep it short and personal.

Reply to a critical review

Hi [Name], we're sorry your visit didn't go the way you expected. We'd like to understand what happened and put it right — please call us on +91 00000 00000.
— Dr. Meera Kulkarni, Sunrise Dental Studio

Never mention treatment details in public — patient privacy comes first. Move the conversation to a call.

WhatsApp quick reply for new enquiries

Thanks for messaging Sunrise Dental Studio! To book, please send:
1) Your name
2) What you need help with
3) A day and time that suits you

We'll confirm your slot shortly.
Timings: Mon – Sat: 10:00 am – 2:00 pm, 5:00 pm – 9:00 pm · Sunday: By appointment

Save it in WhatsApp Business → Tools → Quick replies, as "/book".

Appointment reminder (day before)

Hi [Name], a reminder of your appointment at Sunrise Dental Studio tomorrow at [time].
Reply 1 to confirm or 2 to reschedule.
📍 Shop 4, Example Arcade, Andheri West, Mumbai 400058

Reminders the day before are the simplest way to cut no-shows.

6-month recall

Hi [Name], it's been about 6 months since your last visit to Sunrise Dental — time for your check-up and cleaning. Reply with a day that suits you and we'll find a slot.

Send to every patient whose last visit was 6+ months ago. Ten messages a day is enough.
