# Reference pages

Three self-contained pages built with a full design process (written plan, rendered and checked at desktop and phone width). They are the "good" targets in the Haiku Design Trials.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `jelly-watermelon.html` | three.js scene: translucent half-dome jelly with seeds on a plate. Drops in, then squashes and ripples when tapped. |
| `kinara-dental.html` | Clinic landing page where the printed rate card is the hero. One booking action, live open-now status. |
| `velora-dashboard.html` | Founder dashboard that opens with a one-sentence answer, a single KPI strip, an MRR line, plan mix and cohort table. |

Kinara Dental Care and Velora are invented. Their names, prices, reviews, ratings and numbers are placeholders and must not be used for a real business.

The watermelon loads three.js from cdn.jsdelivr.net, and all three load Google Fonts, so they need internet access.
