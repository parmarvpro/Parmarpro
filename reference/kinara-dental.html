Questions people ask before booking

Will it hurt?

We numb the area with gel before any injection and use a fine needle, so most patients feel only a light pinch. You can stop us at any point by raising your hand.

How much will my treatment cost?

Starting prices are on the rate card above. After your check-up and X-ray, we give you the exact cost in writing before any treatment starts.

Can I pay in EMIs?

Yes. Implants and clear aligners can be paid in monthly instalments. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share a plan for your treatment.

How long does the first visit take?

About 30 minutes: a check-up, a digital X-ray if needed, and a written treatment plan with prices.
