FAQ

Questions clinics ask us

Why is the preview free?

Because showing beats telling. We'd rather spend a few hours building something real for your clinic than ask you for a meeting. If it's good, some clinics ask us to run it. If it isn't useful, it cost you nothing.

Is my preview public?

No. It's on a private link that search engines are told not to index, and it's clearly marked as a concept by Parmarpro, not your official site. Ask and we'll remove it within 24 hours.

Can I just use the plan myself?

Yes. The scripts, the checklist and the website design are yours to use. If you'd rather do it in-house, we're glad it helped.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your clinic's name and the site is yours. If you ever stop working with us, you keep it.

What do you need from me?

A 15-minute call, any photos or logo you have, and manager access to your Google Business Profile — added from your own account. We never ask for passwords.

Do you work with other kinds of businesses?

Right now we focus only on dental clinics. Doing one thing well is the point.
